SUPPORT & FAQ

Need help piloting Railway Runner or setting up your profile? Contact support or check the guides below.

How do I control the runner in Railway Runner?

Use Arrow Keys or WASD. Up/W jumps over barricades, Down/S slides under gates, and Left/Right (or A/D) changes tracks. Escape pauses.

Is my high score saved?

Yes! Your personal best score and gold coin records are automatically saved in local storage.
